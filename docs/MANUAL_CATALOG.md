# Recovered manual inventory

This release inventories the original recorded public manual sources, not eight illustration selections and not a new crawl. It draws from primary/secondary/supplemental download manifests, emergency/IT staging metadata and a bounded guide-directory metadata check. Private, locally supplied documents without public provenance are not published.

- **39 distinct manual/reference candidates; 21 auto-eligible PDFs; 18 manual-action entries.**
- Eligible originals: **54,917,273 bytes** (52.37 MiB). All 21 have historical original-download SHA-256 and byte counts. These are locally computed integrity pins, not publisher signatures.
- All manual candidates with known original sizes: **65,352,337 bytes**; **16 original sizes unknown**. Two known-size PDFs remain manual-action because rights/download permission is not established.
- **23 archive/reader entries** are recorded separately in JSON but never downloaded by this tool. They include multi-GB collections. Models, actual map data and indexes are not manual downloads.
- **8 generated/support entries** are not distinct public manual originals. Three failed alternate URLs for already-listed titles are preserved separately; five other failed historical candidates are included in the 39.
- **0 dangerous operational manual exclusions identified in the recovered manual metadata.** This is not a full audit of mixed-topic archive contents; those archives are outside automated scope. General preparedness, first aid and secure-storage references are not weapon-construction instructions.
- Current bounded checks (2026-09-13 UTC): 20 eligible publisher PDF headers accessible under robots rules; WHO access check failed. No bypass. Header checks are not full-file hash verification. Manual-action URLs were not re-probed. Published pins remain historical 2026-09-11 original hashes.

Public-domain claims apply only to federal-authored portions with third-party notices retained. IFRC/FAO/WHO and iFixit rights are not public domain; preserve attribution and noncommercial/other restrictions. MSF and ICRC require manual rights/download review here. HTML references were blocked or transformed in the original library; their transformed local hashes are deliberately not misrepresented as original publisher hashes. Open them manually and review publisher terms. Never upload the PDFs to this repository.
